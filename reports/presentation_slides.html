Slide 02 · Problem Statement

The Environmental & Operational Crisis

  • Global Impact: Tropical and temperate deforestation contributes 12–15% of annual greenhouse gas emissions and causes irreversible loss of biological diversity.
  • Operational Bottleneck: Forest enforcement brigades and ranger teams operate under severe budgetary constraints and cannot monitor millions of hectares simultaneously.
  • Asymmetric Penalty: A false negative (missing an emerging hotspot) permits illegal logging roads to penetrate deep forest core areas, making subsequent eviction ecologically and politically impossible.
  • The Mission: Shift jurisdictional governance from reactive satellite disaster response to preventative machine learning prioritization.
Slide 03 · The Baseline Paradox

The "Persistence Trap" in Climate Data

Annual forest loss has an extreme lag-1 autocorrelation of ρ = 0.925. Mirroring this year's loss as next year's prediction (yt+1 = yt) yields an apparent R² of 0.9452 and MAE of 0.0969%.

How Flawed AI Fails

Most ML submissions fall into two traps: they either memorize persistence (acting as useless 1-year delayed mirrors) or use random K-fold cross-validation which shuffles future years into the past—causing catastrophic data leakage and illusory 99% accuracy scores.

Our Scientific Standard

CanopyIntel set an unyielding benchmark: can machine learning extract real causal signal that statistically beats persistence on a strictly quarantined 2023–2024 holdout test set?

Slide 04 · Methodology & Math

Residual Log-Ratio Learning

Instead of regressing on raw levels, we transform the prediction problem into multiplicative trajectory residuals.

zt = ln [ (yt+1 + ε) / (yt + ε) ]   ⟹   ŷt+1 = (yt + ε) · exp(ẑt) − ε

Why Log-Ratio Learning Wins

• Symmetrizes positive and negative accelerations.
• Strips out dominant autoperistence levels, forcing tree nodes to split on turning points.
• Naturally enforces strictly positive loss bounds without clipping.

Physical Mass Balance

Multi-year autoregressive rollouts strictly conserve physical hectares:

Areat+1 = Areat − Losst + Gaint
